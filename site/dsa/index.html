DSA: Single market for digital services.

All 93 articles, 156 recitals in English. Official EU text (English). Text as published in the Official Journal (OJ L 277, 27.10.2022, p. 1). Not legal advice.

This Regulation shall apply from 17 February 2024.Article 93: more dates and parts

When it applies, in the act's own words (Article 93). In force and applying are different: the act can be in force before its rules apply.

2. This Regulation shall apply from 17 February 2024.
However, Article 24(2), (3) and (6), Article 33(3) to (6), Article 37(7), Article 40(13), Article 43 and Sections 4, 5 and 6 of Chapter IV shall apply from 16 November 2022.
