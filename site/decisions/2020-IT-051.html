Fines/ Italy/ 2020/IT/051

UniCredit S.p.A.

Fine€600,000
Country
Italy
Decided
10 June 2020
Sector
Finance, Insurance and Consulting
Regulator
Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (Italy)
01

Summary

UniCredit notified the Garante in July 2017 of an intrusion, between April 2016 and July 2017, that gave unauthorised access to data of about 762,000 people through the Speedy Arena application, using credentials of staff of an external partner. The Garante found weak authorisation profiles, inadequate and wrongly retained tracking logs, no alerts and no internal audits. The case was decided under the old Italian Privacy Code (not the GDPR). It fined the bank EUR 600,000 in total, made up of three separate fines, and rejected the bank's defence that the partner's misuse of credentials and a software bug excused it.
