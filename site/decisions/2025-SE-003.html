What happened

The Equality Ombudsman (DO) ran a web form for tips and complaints about discrimination. An analytics tool from a processor was meant to mask free-text entries, but the masking did not work on the form's summary page. Personal data, some of it sensitive or covered by secrecy, was therefore transferred to and stored with the processor for about a year (1 Sept 2020 to 17 Sept 2021). IMY found that DO had not taken appropriate technical and organisational measures under Article 32(1). It rejected a reprimand as too lenient, rated the infringement as low severity, found no aggravating or mitigating factors, and imposed an administrative fine of SEK 100,000.
