Fines/ Greece/ 2025/GR/020

National Bank of Greece

  • Accountability
  • Data subject rights
  • Transparency
Fine€200,000
Country
Greece
Decided
9 January 2025
Sector
Finance, Insurance and Consulting
Regulator
Hellenic Data Protection Authority (HDPA)
01

Summary

The Authority examined seven complaints against the National Bank of Greece about late or missing responses to data subjects' access requests, mostly for recordings of phone calls after fraud incidents. Replies came months late, and the bank blamed remote-working arrangements and request volumes. For the first complainant, whose recordings were withheld while the bank used them in a court dispute, it fined the bank EUR 20,000 under Article 15. For the other six complaints it issued a reprimand under Articles 12(3)-(4) and 15. In its own-initiative audit it found that the bank had no adequate procedures for handling access requests (Articles 12(2) and 25(1)) and fined it EUR 200,000.

Summary by Fino, read in parts of the regulator’s own decision. Every fact here was checked word for word against those parts (2026-10-11).
