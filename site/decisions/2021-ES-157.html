Fines/ Spain/ 2021/ES/157

Spanish Data Protection Authority (AEPD) · Date not published

Vodafone España, S.A.U.

About: Accountability, Data subject rights, International transfers, Processor obligations

What happened next. Reduced in court, not final: on 29 Oct 2024 the National High Court (Audiencia Nacional) partly upheld Vodafone's challenge and cut the four fines to EUR 3,000,000 (Arts. 28 and 24 GDPR), EUR 50,000 (Art. 44 GDPR), EUR 30,000 (Art. 21 LSSICE, downgraded to a minor breach) and EUR 1,500,000 (Art. 48.1.b LGT): EUR 4,580,000 in total; it also cancelled the order to bring the processing into line within six months. On 23 Apr 2025 the Supreme Court agreed to hear an appeal by Vodafone (whether Vodafone was the controller of its partners' marketing) and one by the State (whether the AEPD may order compliance in general terms) (ATS 4230/2025, recurso de casación 1866/2025); Fino has found no Supreme Court ruling yet, so the amount may still change. What happened

The Spanish DPA examined 191 complaints (162 after leaving out 29 with faulty data) about marketing calls, SMS and emails made on behalf of Vodafone España, including to people who had opted out or were on advertising exclusion (Robinson) lists. It found that Vodafone failed to control its processors (Arts. 28 and 24 GDPR), made international transfers in breach of Art. 44 GDPR, breached Art. 21 LSSICE on commercial electronic communications, and breached Art. 48.1.b LGT read with Art. 21 GDPR and Art. 23 LOPDGDD on the right to object to unwanted calls. It imposed four fines (EUR 4,000,000, 2,000,000, 150,000 and 2,000,000, EUR 8,150,000 in total) and ordered Vodafone to bring its processing into line with the GDPR and LOPDGDD within six months.
