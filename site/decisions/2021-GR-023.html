Fines/ Greece/ 2021/GR/023

Organization of Urban Transport of Thessaloniki (OASTH)

  • Transparency
Fine€10,000
Country
Greece
Decided
7 September 2021
Sector
Transportation and Energy
Regulator
HDPA (Greece)
01

Summary

Twelve former shareholders of OASTH (the Thessaloniki urban transport organisation) complained that its president named them at a press conference and in a press release listing 29 former shareholders, with their stakes, purchase prices and contract dates. They also complained that their access/objection requests went unanswered. The Authority found that publishing the names of two shareholders, who had made no claims, breached Articles 4 and 5 of Law 2472/1997, and that failing to answer the requests breached Articles 12 and 13 of that law. It fined OASTH EUR 3,000 and EUR 7,000 (EUR 10,000 in total) and warned it to comply with the GDPR going forward. The case was decided under the old Law 2472/1997, not the GDPR.
