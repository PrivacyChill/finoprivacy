What happened

An employee of KEAT asked twice in June and July 2023 for the CCTV footage of an incident in the centre's courtyard and received no answer. Footage from only one of two cameras was supplied late, after the HDPA intervened, and it had been processed (zoomed). The HDPA found breaches of the access right (Art. 15 with Art. 12(3)) and of Art. 24, since staff were not trained to handle such requests. It fined the controller EUR 5,000 for the access breach, reprimanded it for Art. 24, and ordered it to supply the requested footage within 15 days.
