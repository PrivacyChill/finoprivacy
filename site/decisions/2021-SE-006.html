Fines/ Sweden/ 2021/SE/006

Region Värmland

  • Data principles
  • Transparency
FineSEK250,000≈ €24,900
Country
Sweden
Decided
7 June 2021
Sector
Public Sector and Education
Regulator
Data Protection Authority of Sweden (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten)
01

Summary

After a newspaper reported that millions of recorded calls to the 1177 healthcare advice line were openly exposed online, IMY opened supervision of the regional boards using the service, including Region Värmland. IMY found that callers were not informed that their phone number and municipality ID were collected so calls could be routed to the care provider MedHelp AB. Missing were the controller's identity and contact details, purpose, legal basis, access right and the DPO contact. IMY found breaches of Articles 5(1)(a) and 13 and imposed an administrative fine of SEK 250,000. The data minimisation and legal basis for the processing were found to be fine.
