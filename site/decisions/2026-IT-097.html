What happened

A woman complained about an article in Il Fatto Quotidiano on a 2021 cable car accident. It named her and gave detailed information about her hospital stay, although she was only a witness in the criminal case. The publisher de-indexed the article and later removed it, but argued it had acted lawfully. The Garante found that naming her and the detailed health account breached the principles of lawfulness, fairness and minimisation and the essentiality of information rule. It fined the publisher EUR 23,750 and ordered publication of the decision.
