What happened

Following several investigations completed in December 2024, the Romanian authority found that Vodafone Romania repeatedly failed to keep customer data confidential because staff or processors did not follow internal data-handling policies. Incidents included sending an invoice photo to a third party, not using BCC in group emails, sharing a screenshot via WhatsApp, and sending an invoice to the wrong person. The controller was fined RON 74,526 (about EUR 15,000) for breaching Article 32(4) with Article 32(1)(b) GDPR. The operator has paid the fine.
