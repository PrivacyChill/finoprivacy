What happened

A customer complained that Vodafone Panafon did not properly answer her repeated requests for access to recordings of her calls about a technical fault. The company rejected the first two requests as incomplete, asked her to specify dates, and finally supplied 18 recordings from 25-11-2021 onward, while earlier calls were lost under its 12-month retention. The Authority found infringements of Articles 12(1)-(4), 15 and 18 GDPR. It imposed a fine of EUR 30,000 and ordered the company to take technical and organisational measures, including better staff training, to handle data subject requests properly and on time.
