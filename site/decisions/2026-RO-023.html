What happened

The Romanian DPA, acting as lead authority after a referral from the French CNIL, investigated Ascendex Technology SRL over an erasure request. The company took about 12 months to handle the complainant's request, never sent a final reply and did not justify the delay; for other data subjects the delay reached 37 months. The authority found breaches of Articles 12(1) and (3) read with Article 17(1) GDPR and fined the company 57,839 lei (about EUR 11,000). It also ordered the company to reply properly to the complainant and similar data subjects and to train staff regularly on handling data subject requests.
