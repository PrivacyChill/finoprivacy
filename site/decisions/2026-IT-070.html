Fines/ Italy/ 2026/IT/070

Lidl Italia S.r.l.

  • Data principles
  • Data subject rights
  • Transparency
Fine€43,000
Country
Italy
Decided
14 May 2026
Sector
Industry and Commerce
Regulator
Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante)
01

Summary

A customer asked Lidl Italia by certified email, within hours of an incident at a store in Arco (TN), for access to the CCTV images of him and for their retention. Lidl insisted on its own request form and then deleted the footage, and replied by registered post, not the PEC the customer used. The reply also omitted the right to complain to the authority. The Garante found breaches of fairness, facilitation of rights, access and restriction, and fined Lidl EUR 43,000, also ordering publication of the order on its website.

Summary by Fino, read in the regulator’s own decision. Every fact here was checked word for word against it (2026-10-12).
