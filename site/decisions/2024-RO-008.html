Fines/ Romania/ 2024/RO/008

Vodafone România SA

  • Data subject rights
  • Transparency
FineRON14,930≈ €3,000
Country
Romania
Announced
16 September 2024
Sector
Media, Telecoms and Broadcasting
Regulator
National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP)
01

Summary

Following a complaint, the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP) investigated Vodafone România SA for failing to respond to a data subject's request to exercise their rights of access and erasure within the statutory 30-day period. The investigation established that the controller only provided a response after the supervisory authority intervened. Consequently, the ANSPDCP fined Vodafone România SA 14,930 lei (equivalent to EUR 3,000) for infringements of Articles 12(3), 15, and 17 GDPR. In addition, the authority imposed a corrective measure ordering the company to adopt an internal procedure for handling data subject requests under Articles 12-22 GDPR and to regularly train its staff.

Summary by Fino, read in the regulator’s own press release. Every fact here was checked word for word against it (2026-10-24).
