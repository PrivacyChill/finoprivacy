What happened

The Romanian authority investigated UiPath SRL after it notified a personal data breach: the data of about 600,000 users of its Academy platform was exposed on a third-party website for about 10 days because storage settings allowed unauthorised access. The authority found breaches of Articles 25 and 32 GDPR, acting as lead authority under Article 60. It fined the company RON 346,598 (EUR 70,000) and ordered it to set up a regular process for testing and evaluating the effectiveness of its security measures.
