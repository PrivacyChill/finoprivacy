Fines/ Italy/ 2021/IT/054

Planet Group Spa

  • Accountability
  • Data principles
  • Data subject rights
  • Unlawful processing
Fine€80,000
Country
Italy
Decided
11 March 2021
Sector
Industry and Commerce
Regulator
Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante)
01

Summary

After hundreds of complaints about unwanted promotional calls made on behalf of TIM, the Garante inspected the call centre Planet Group. It found the company called 47,981 'referenced' numbers outside TIM's lists without a legal basis or consent, kept calling people who had objected, called some people very insistently, and had inadequate privacy-by-design. The Garante treated Planet Group as de facto (joint) controller, imposed a permanent limitation of the processing with compliance orders, and fined it EUR 80,000, with publication of the decision.

Summary by Fino, read in parts of the regulator’s own decision. Every fact here was checked word for word against those parts (2026-10-12).
