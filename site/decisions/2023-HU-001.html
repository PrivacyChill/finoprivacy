What happened

Digi reported a data breach in September 2019: an attacker exploited a years-old known vulnerability in its content management system on digi.hu and reached databases of about 322,000 people, mostly a test database that should have been deleted after use. The authority had fined Digi HUF 100 million in 2020; after the CJEU (C-77/21) and the Budapest Tribunal found no purpose-limitation violation, the authority repeated the procedure on the sanctions. It found violations of storage limitation (Art. 5(1)(e)) and security of processing (Art. 32(1)-(2)), ordered Digi to review all its databases for the need for encryption, and fined it HUF 80 million (4/5 of the original). The decision is to be published with the controller's identity.
