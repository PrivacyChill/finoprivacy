01

Summary

A law firm article reports that Malta's data protection commissioner fined the Lands Authority EUR 5,000 on 18 February 2019. The Authority's website lacked appropriate security measures, so over 10 GB of personal data, mostly sensitive and including correspondence with individuals, could be found through a simple Google search. The Authority did not appeal and has begun penetration testing and moved its data to servers run by the Malta Information Technology Agency. The article is a secondary commentary, not the decision itself, and cites no GDPR articles.

Summary by Fino, read in a law firm’s article (gvzh.com.mt), not in the regulator’s own document. Every fact here was checked word for word against that article (2026-10-12).
