Lazio Region

Fine€100,000
Country
Italy
Decided
15 September 2022
Sector
Public Sector and Education
Regulator
Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante)
01

Summary

A mother complained that the Rieti local health authority had sent her daughter, who died in 1995, an invitation to a cervical cancer screening programme. The Garante found that the regional screening database (SIPSOweb), run by Lazio Region, held outdated data, and that the Region had not correctly identified the roles of the parties involved, the legal bases for the processing, or the information to give to data subjects. It declared the processing unlawful, ordered the Region to correct roles, purposes, legal bases and information notices within 90 days, and fined it EUR 100,000. The decision is also to be published on the Garante's website.
