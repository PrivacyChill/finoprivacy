What happened

The Romanian DPA finished an investigation into Unicredit Bank SA after a petition from an individual. The bank sent erroneous insurance policy expiry notifications to a large number of customers, disclosing other customers' data because of a file-processing error with improper manual handling. The authority found failures in technical and organisational security measures and a late breach notification beyond 72 hours. It imposed two fines totalling 62,714 lei (EUR 12,000 equivalent): EUR 10,000 equivalent for Article 32 and EUR 2,000 equivalent for Article 33.
