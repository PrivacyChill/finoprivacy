What happened

As part of the EDPB's 2023 coordinated action on the designation and position of data protection officers, the Greek authority sent a questionnaire to 31 public bodies, including the Ministry of Rural Development and Food. The Ministry did not answer the questionnaire or a reminder, and it was found to have had no DPO from 4 August 2022 to 20 June 2023 because the previous contract expired and was not renewed for lack of funds. The authority found infringements of Article 31 (cooperation with the supervisory authority) and Article 37 (DPO designation). It imposed a fine of EUR 5,000 for Article 31 and EUR 20,000 for Article 37, EUR 25,000 in total.
