What happened

Parents of a pupil injured at school complained that the school held a video of the incident, filmed with a phone from a screen showing CCTV footage and handed to a private investigator hired by the insurer. The Garante found that the school's CCTV system, running since 2019 including in daytime with minors and staff present, had no valid legal basis (legitimate interest not available), breached the labour-law safeguards of Art. 4 of Law 300/1970, lacked adequate transparency and had no DPIA. It declared the processing unlawful and fined the congregation's province EUR 12,000. No further corrective measures were ordered because the cameras had been switched off and litigation was pending.
