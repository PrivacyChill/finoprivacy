Fines/ Cyprus/ 2019/CY/005

Company (newspaper)

Fine€10,000
Country
Cyprus
Reported
1 March 2019
Sector
Media, Telecoms and Broadcasting
Regulator
Commissioner for Personal Data Protection
01

Summary

A law firm's news article reports two separate decisions of the Cyprus data protection Commissioner. One fined a State Hospital EUR 5,000 for failing to satisfy a patient's access request because her medical file could not be located. In the other, a newspaper was fined EUR 10,000 for publishing, in print and online, the names and pictures of two police officers, and a photograph of a third. The Commissioner held that the aim could be reached with initials, blurred faces or distant photographs, and weighed EU Court of Justice case law on freedom of expression versus privacy.

Summary by Fino, read in a law firm’s article (agplaw.com), not in the regulator’s own document. Every fact here was checked word for word against that article (2026-10-12).
