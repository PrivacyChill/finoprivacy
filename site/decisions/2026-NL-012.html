Uber

Non-compliance with general data processing principles

Regulator

01

Summary

The DPA fined Uber €824.99 million for automatically deactivating drivers’ accounts without human intervention, in violation of Article 22 GDPR, and for insufficient transparency.

Summary from the GDPRhub page for this decision, written by its volunteers, not by Fino. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

02

Rules involved

  • Art. 14Information to be provided where personal data have not been obtained from the data subject
