Tools/AI Act check

Tools · EU AI Act · text as amended in July 2026

Is your AI system banned, high-risk or neither?

1. What is your role?
2. Does it do any of these? Tick any that apply. These are banned (Article 5).
3. Where is it used? Tick any that apply. These are the high-risk areas.
3b. In that area, does it profile people? And does it only do a narrow task? An Annex III system is not high-risk if it poses no significant risk, for example because it only does a narrow procedural or preparatory task, unless it profiles people (Article 6(3)).
4. Does it do any of these? Transparency duties (Article 50).
