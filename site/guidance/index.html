About this list

Guidelines, opinions and binding decisions of the European Data Protection Board, and the Article 29 Working Party guidelines the Board endorsed. Not a complete list: see known_gaps. Each page shows the record as Fino read it in the original, with the link to it. Not legal advice.

What is not here

  • Fino holds 27 EDPB and Working Party documents. The Board has published many more guidelines, recommendations and opinions; the others are not yet in Fino.
  • Guidelines 05/2022 on facial recognition in law enforcement and Binding Decision 1/2026 (Belgian authority) were not built: their numbered paragraphs or their title and date could not be read reliably from the PDF.
  • Documents the Board adopted for public consultation only (draft guidelines) are not in Fino, except Guidelines 01/2025 on pseudonymisation, which is marked as a draft.
  • Opinions under Article 64 other than Opinions 22/2024 and 28/2024, and binding decisions other than the five that Fino's decisions cite, are not in Fino.
  • Where the Board has published a corrected version later than the copy Fino read, status_note says so; Fino has not compared the texts.
