How to read this page

Opinion
An opinion answers a request made to the Board on a matter of general application or with effects in more than one Member State (Article 64(2) GDPR). It is not law itself.
Version and status
Documents of the Board are often adopted for public consultation first and then adopted again as a final version. Fino shows the version it read and what that document says about itself.
Extracts
Copied word for word from the document, each with its paragraph or section. They are a selection, and Fino has not shortened or reworded them.

Fino holds 27 documents of the Board and the Working Party, not all of them. Fino has not checked whether the Board has published a later version than the one it read.
