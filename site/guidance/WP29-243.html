01

In one line

Article 29 Working Party, endorsed by the European Data Protection Board, WP 243 rev.01, adopted 5 April 2017, section 3.5, "Conflict of interests": “This entails in particular that the DPO cannot hold a position within the organisation that leads him or her to determine the purposes and the means of the processing of personal data.”

Fino’s one-line citation of the document, word for word. It says what the document says, not what it means for an organisation.

How to read this page

Article 29 Working Party guidelines
The Article 29 Working Party was the advisory group of the EU's data protection authorities before the GDPR applied. Its guidelines on the GDPR were endorsed by the European Data Protection Board. The Board endorsed it on 25 May 2018.
Version and status
Documents of the Board are often adopted for public consultation first and then adopted again as a final version. Fino shows the version it read and what that document says about itself.
Extracts
Copied word for word from the document, each with its paragraph or section. They are a selection, and Fino has not shortened or reworded them.

Fino holds 27 documents of the Board and the Working Party, not all of them. Fino has not checked whether the Board has published a later version than the one it read.
