About this list

Judgments of the Court of Justice of the European Union on the GDPR and related data-protection rules, and the very famous earlier cases (Directive 95/46/EC and related texts). Not a complete list: see known_gaps. Each page shows the record as Fino read it in the original, with the link to it. Not legal advice.

What is not here

  • Fino holds 53 judgments. The Court of Justice has decided many more cases on the GDPR; the others are not yet in Fino.
  • Judgments delivered after 4 September 2025 were not searched for.
  • Orders of the Court, opinions of Advocates General and judgments of the General Court are not in Fino.
  • Judgments on the earlier Directive 95/46/EC, the ePrivacy Directive or the Data Retention Directive are in Fino only when they are a very famous earlier case, each checked against the judgment (they are cited in the EU Handbook of 2018 or on Fino's key-cases pages); the others are not.
  • National court rulings, such as an English Court of Appeal case on the meaning of personal data, and cases of the European Court of Human Rights are not in Fino. The reuse terms of the Court of Human Rights are narrower than those of the EU.
