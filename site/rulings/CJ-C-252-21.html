Meta Platforms and Others v Bundeskartellamt: a competition authority, special categories and consent

Popular name: Meta v Bundeskartellamt

  • Supervisory authority powers
  • Special categories of data

Official titleReference for a preliminary ruling – Protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data – Regulation (EU) 2016/679 – Social networks – Abuse of a dominant position by the operator of such a network – Abuse which entails the processing of the personal data of the users of that network as provided for in its general terms of use – Powers of a competition authority of a Member State to find that processing is not consistent with that regulation – Reconciliation with the powers of the national data protection supervisory authorities – Article 4(3) TEU – Principle of sincere cooperation – Points (a) to (f) of the first subparagraph of Article 6(1) of Regulation 2016/679 – Whether the processing is lawful – Article 9(1) and (2) – Processing of special categories of personal data – Article 4(11) – Concept of ‘consent’

01

In one line

Court of Justice (Grand Chamber), 4 July 2023, case C-252/21, operative part, point 8: “Point (a) of the first subparagraph of Article 6(1) and Article 9(2)(a) of Regulation 2016/679 must be interpreted as meaning that the fact that the operator of an online social network holds a dominant position on the market for online social networks does not, as such, preclude the users of such a network from being able validly to consent, within the meaning of Article 4(11) of that regulation, to the processing of their personal data by that operator.”

How to read this page

Reference for a preliminary ruling
A national court asks the Court of Justice how a rule of EU law must be read. The Court answers the question; the national court then decides its own case.
Operative part
The Court’s answer, set out at the end of the judgment. The paragraphs before it give the reasons.
ECLI
The European Case Law Identifier: a standard code for citing a judgment, the same on every official site.
Extracts
Copied word for word from the judgment, each with the number of the paragraph it comes from. They are a selection, not the whole reasoning, and Fino has not shortened or reworded them.

Fino holds 53 judgments of the Court of Justice, not all of them. Judgments delivered after 4 September 2025 were not searched for. Fino has not checked whether a later judgment has refined or replaced this one.
