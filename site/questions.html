Questions

Questions a DPO is asked

The questions a data protection officer (DPO) is asked, answered from real decisions, one card each. The cards are also posted on LinkedIn.

Not legal advice.

6 questions

Card, 19 Nov 2026· Staff

Someone left. Is their mailbox still open?

What the APD decided: In principle, close a leaver’s mailbox: an automatic reply for about a month, then delete it.

In April 2026 Belgium’s APD fined an accountancy firm €8,500 for keeping a former employee’s mailbox open for at least 21 months.

APD decision 86/2026, 23 April 2026. Checked 23 Oct 2026.

Read the decision: Someone left. Is their mailbox still open?

Card, 17 Nov 2026· AI and new tech1 decision

A recruiter makes the final call. Is it still automated?

Spain's AEPD, in a warning: Human involvement must be effective: the automated result must not in fact decide.

A company planned an AI tool to score job candidates and said the final decision is always human. A warning, no fine. EU regulators’ (EDPB) sixth coordinated enforcement action, from 2027, is on this.

AEPD warning EXP202600427 (undated); EDPB news, 11 Nov 2026. Checked 12 Nov 2026.

Read the explainer: A recruiter makes the final call. Is it still automated?

Card, 12 Nov 2026· People’s rights and health

Can a doctor reply to a review with a diagnosis?

What the DSB decided: No: he could have answered the review without mentioning the diagnosis.

In 2023 Austria’s DSB fined a gynaecologist €10,000 for revealing a patient’s diagnosis in a public reply to her online review.

DSB decision 2023-0.420.407, 29 June 2023. Checked 23 Oct 2026.

Read the decision: Can a doctor reply to a review with a diagnosis?

Card, 10 Nov 2026· Websites and marketing

Why was a €10,000 GDPR fine cut to €1?

What the court decided: The fine was out of proportion; a symbolic euro is appropriate.

Belgium’s regulator (APD) fined railway SNCB €10,000 in 2022 for a marketing email sent without a valid legal basis and with no mention of the right to object; the appeal changed only the amount.

APD decision 71/2022; Market Court (Brussels) 2022/AR/723, 14 June 2023. Checked 23 Oct 2026.

Read the decision: Why was a €10,000 GDPR fine cut to €1?

Card, 5 Nov 2026· Security and breaches

Can everyone on your group email see the others’ addresses?

What the ANSPDCP decided: Not hiding the addresses, for example with BCC, breached the GDPR’s security rule.

In 2024 Romania’s ANSPDCP fined Vodafone Romania 24,870.5 lei (about €5,000) for client emails that showed every address.

ANSPDCP press release, 28 Oct 2024. Checked 23 Oct 2026.

Read the decision: Can everyone on your group email see the others’ addresses?

Card, 3 Nov 2026· Staff

Can you write down why an employee is off sick?

What the AP decided: Naming illnesses is not needed to help staff back to work, so it is banned.

In March 2020 the Dutch AP fined CP&A €15,000: its list of 25 sick staff, with their illnesses, sat in a Google Drive file that opened with no login.

AP decision on CP&A B.V., 24 March 2020. Checked 23 Oct 2026.

Read the decision: Can you write down why an employee is off sick?
