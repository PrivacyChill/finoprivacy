What you’ll find here, and what you won’t

On Fino

  • Fines and other decisions of the data protection regulators of the EU, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein and the UK, from 2018 to this week.
  • For each one: who it is against, how much, which GDPR articles, the date, and a link to the regulator’s own document.
  • Fines that a court later annulled or cut, marked when it’s known.
  • The GDPR and the AI Act in full, each article linked to the decisions that apply it.
  • Short explainers that answer common questions with real cases.

Not on Fino (yet)

  • Legal advice. Fino shows what regulators did; it can’t tell you what to do in your case.
  • Every decision ever made. Some regulators publish only a few, and Fino says where the gaps are.
  • Court cases in their own right, and news commentary.
  • Laws beyond the GDPR and the AI Act. The ePrivacy rules on cookies come next.

From the source, checked twice.

Fino started from two public collections, the CMS Enforcement Tracker and GDPRhub. Now it goes back to each regulator’s own decision, one at a time, so every fact can be traced.

  1. Fino reads the originalThe regulator’s own decision, in its own language. Every fact is noted with the decision’s exact words.
  2. A second reading checks itAn independent check has to agree, and a program confirms every quote is really in the document. What stays unclear, Lorenzo decides.
  3. You can check it tooEvery decision links to the original, so you never have to take Fino’s word for it.
