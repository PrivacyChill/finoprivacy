Where the facts come from

  • Sourced. Each decision page links to its source and says whether Fino read the original: 1,273 of 5,058 are read from an official document, in full or in part.
  • Summaries. Where Fino wrote one, it is nearly always from an official document, with AI help; 9 come from a news report or a law firm’s article, and the page says so.
  • Gaps stated. Each country page says what Fino holds; the connector reports a missing fact as “not in Fino’s data”, not as “no”.
  • Appeals shown. Where Fino has recorded an appeal or a court ruling, the page says so. No recorded appeal does not prove there was none.
  • Laws copied. Law texts are copied word for word from the official source.
  • Corrections public. What Fino got wrong, and what the page says now.
  • No private names. Private people and sole traders are described, never named, except in the popular name of a very famous court case, such as Schrems II or Lindqvist.
