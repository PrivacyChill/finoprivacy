Who made Fino

  • Going back to the originalFino starts from the CMS Enforcement Tracker and GDPRhub and is going back to each regulator’s own document, one decision at a time. Each page says whether it has been checked.
  • The law, and what it meansThe GDPR and the AI Act, article by article, each linked to the decisions that apply it, and explainers built on real cases.
  • Not everything, not adviceSome regulators publish little, so a dim country is not a safe one. Fino shows what regulators did, not what to do in your case.
