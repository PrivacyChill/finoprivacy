05

How each Fino document is built

  • Traced. Every item links to the article or official guidance it comes from.
  • Dated. The law text and guidance it uses carry their dates.
  • Assumptions stated. Each says at the top who it is for and what it does not cover.
  • Official sources only. The law’s text, European Data Protection Board (EDPB) guidance, and national regulators’ guidance and models, where their reuse terms allow.
  • Backed by decisions. Where a regulator fined an organisation for a missing item, the decision is linked.
  • Corrected in public. Changes after publication are listed on About.
