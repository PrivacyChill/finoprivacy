Documents · Checklist · Italy, Germany

Do you need a DPIA?

A screening checklist for a data protection impact assessment. For each line tick yes, no or unknown. Write who you asked.

Also in: Italiano · Deutsch

For

A controller in Italy or Germany deciding whether a processing needs a data protection impact assessment (DPIA), and the professional who advises it.

Not covered

The DPIA itself. Other countries’ national lists (Article 35(4)). A match on no list is not the same as no DPIA needed.

Law as of

GDPR as in force 7 Nov 2026; WP 248 rev.01 of 4 Oct 2017; Garante provision 467 of 11 Oct 2018; DSK Muss-Liste version 1.1 of 17 Oct 2018

Step 3. Italy: the Garante list (provision 467 of 11 October 2018, Annex 1)

The Garante says the list covers only processing under the consistency mechanism and is not exhaustive. Item 5 covers employee monitoring by video surveillance and geolocation. 05

What the result means

Any yes in Step 1, or a match in Step 3 or 4: a DPIA is required (Art. 35(3); national lists under Art. 35(4)). Two or more yes in Step 2: in most cases a DPIA is required (WP 248, p. 11). Article 35(1): the assessment is carried out before the processing starts.

WP 248 (p. 8): "In cases where it is not clear whether a DPIA is required, the WP29 recommends that a DPIA is carried out nonetheless".

WP 248 (p. 12): where processing meets two or more criteria in Step 2 but the controller considers it not likely to result in a high risk, "the controller should justify and document the reasons for not carrying out a DPIA, and include/record the views of the data protection officer".

The lists are not exhaustive. No match is not the same as no DPIA needed. Other national lists exist (Art. 35(4)); this checklist covers Italy and Germany only.
