Laws/EUDPR

EUDPR: Data protection rules for the EU institutions.

Regulation (EU) 2018/1725 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 October 2018 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data by the Union institutions, bodies, offices and agencies and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Regulation (EC) No 45/2001 and Decision No 1247/2002/EC

All 101 articles, 89 recitals in English. Official EU text (English). Text as published in the Official Journal (OJ L 295, 21.11.2018, p. 39). Not legal advice.

Law

Version
OriginalOJ L 295, 21.11.2018, p. 39
In force from
11 December 2018Article 101, the act's own entry-into-force article

When it applies. In force, and so applies, from 11 December 2018 (Article 101(1): twentieth day after publication on 21 November 2018); applies to processing by Eurojust from 12 December 2019 (Article 101(2)).
