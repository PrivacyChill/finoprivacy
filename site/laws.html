The privacy laws of Europe

The EU and UK rules on personal data, and the laws around them: cookies, AI, data sharing, online platforms and cybersecurity. The GDPR comes with the decisions that apply it, article by article, with the fines attached, and the AI Act is there in full. Two German and two Italian laws are there in their original language, and 22 EU and UK laws in English. A few more are coming.
